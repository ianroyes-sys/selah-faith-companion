Selah

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Selah is a faith companion app built to be as private as a journal. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device, what is stored on our servers when you choose to use community features, and what we never do with your data.

No account, no email, no login

Selah has no sign-up. We never ask for your email address, phone number, or real identity. When you use community features, your device generates a random identifier and a random secret that together act as your anonymous identity. Neither can be linked back to you personally.

What stays on your device

Never leaves your phone
  • Your journal entries and evening reflections
  • Your prayer vault (open, answered, and released prayers)
  • Your verse memorization deck and reading progress
  • Your conversations with the Companion — AI runs on-device via Apple Intelligence when available, and no chat content is ever sent to a server
  • Spoken prayers — speech is transcribed on-device
  • Calendar context — if you grant calendar access, event titles are read locally to personalize your morning liturgy and are never uploaded
  • Your name — used only to greet you inside the app (it is sent to our server only if you post to the community under your name; you can post anonymously)

What is stored on our servers

Only when you use community features (Prayer Wall, Testimonies, Circles, The Commons, Prayer Relay, daily check-ins), the following is stored on our backend (hosted by Supabase in the United States):

Community data
  • Your random device identifier and a one-way cryptographic hash of your device secret
  • The display name you choose (or "Anonymous")
  • Prayers, testimonies, blessings, and chat messages you post
  • Reactions ("I prayed", "Amen"), circle memberships, and daily heart check-in moods
  • Reports and blocks you file, used solely for moderation
  • A push notification token, only if you enable notifications
  • A presence timestamp used to show how many people are praying right now

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties
  • No analytics trackers or fingerprinting
  • No reading of your journal, vault, or Companion conversations — we technically cannot; they are not on our servers

Moderation

Community spaces are protected by an automated word filter, rate limits, and a report system. Content that receives multiple reports is hidden automatically, and repeat abuse can result in a suspended community identity. You can report or block any author directly in the app.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete your own prayers, testimonies, and messages in the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes all on-device data (journal, vault, deck, chats) permanently.
  • To erase everything tied to your anonymous community identity from our servers, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Selah+ subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment details. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Terms of use: Apple Standard EULA.

Children

Selah is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app.
